How to cite

Cite our paper:

@misc{fountoulakis2026turing,
  author = {Fountoulakis, Kimon and Melnichenko, Maksim and
            Fetrat Qharabagh, Muhammad and Georgantzas, Georgios Alexandros and
            Spyrou, George and Balabanov, Oleg and Georgiou, Vasileios and
            Murray, Riley and Mahoney, Michael W.},
  title  = {Trustworthy Numerical Software in the Age of {AI}:
            What Would {T}uring Do?},
  year   = {2026}
}

In plain text: K. Fountoulakis, M. Melnichenko, M. Fetrat Qharabagh, G. A. Georgantzas, G. Spyrou, O. Balabanov, V. Georgiou, R. Murray, and M. W. Mahoney. Trustworthy Numerical Software in the Age of AI: What Would Turing Do? 2026.

To cite this website:

@misc{tnpg2026website,
  author       = {{The Numerical Proving Ground}},
  title        = {{The Numerical Proving Ground}: assessing reliability of numerical software},
  year         = {2026},
  howpublished = {Website}
}
