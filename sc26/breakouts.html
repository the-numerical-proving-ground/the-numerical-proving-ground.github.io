Opening word

When AI meets the unseen

  • Generative AI learns the typical cases. It struggles with the atypical: rare classes, heavy tails.
  • Where it fails: confident, plausible but wrong details, and familiar assumptions applied outside their regime.
  • So treat its outputs as hypotheses, then verify them like any scientific claim.
