How we handle thoughts

SC26 Birds of a Feather session, Chicago, 2026-11-18

Thoughts appear here at once, without your name. We remove any that break the rules: no names, links or personal details, please. To limit abuse we keep a coded form of your network address and your browser's random id for about 24 hours, and Cloudflare's Turnstile checks that you are not a bot. Thoughts become part of this record (CC BY 4.0); write to us to have one removed.
